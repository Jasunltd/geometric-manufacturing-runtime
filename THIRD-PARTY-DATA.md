# Third-party sources and tools

This study invokes external engineering software and records outputs from
CAD, FEA and CNC-controller toolchains. The source tree includes selected
recorded tool receipts, example inputs and generated/derived observations;
it does **not** bundle a licensed copy of build123d, OCCT, Gmsh, CalculiX,
LinuxCNC, Haas controller software, ReportLab, or their datasets.

The originating software, patents, standards and third-party source materials
retain their own rights and attribution requirements. The source's recorded
structural/controller execution does not establish new third-party
certification, real machining, metrology or production qualification.

The project's original authored software code is staged under MIT; that
license is not an assertion of ownership of independent third-party works.
Complete the final copyright, contributor, toolchain, dataset and provenance
review before public publication.
